# Additional overworld sprite credits

These replacement sprites cover the 24 previously missing CUT-capable species and 26 previously missing SURF-capable species, plus Clefable and Finneon (added in .69).
They are community DS-style artwork, not original PokeWilds 0.8.11 artwork.

Source collection: https://github.com/PokemonWorkshop/GameDataPacks/tree/gen-packs/Gen%209/scarlet-violet/graphics/characters
Original resource pack: https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1101/

## Port adaptation

Frames resized with nearest-neighbor sampling to 16x16, then arranged as six
horizontal frames: left pair, up pair, down pair. Existing right-facing mirroring
and animation timing are retained. Both normal and shiny assets are included.
Original artwork is credited above; port formatting does not imply original authorship.
See provenance.json for species, source URLs, hashes and selected source frames.
