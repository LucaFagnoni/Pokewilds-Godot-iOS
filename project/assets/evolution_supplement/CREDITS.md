# Evolution sprite additions and credits

Existing sprites are retained. Missing front sprites use Gen-2 artwork where available, followed by credited alternative artwork, as requested. The same missing-front fallback is used when viewing the evolved Pokemon in battle and stats.

- AzureKeys/BW3G: Gen-2 Pokemon artwork credited upstream to the COMBOY collection and JaceDeane. See BW3G-README.md for the original credits.
- Rangi42/polishedcrystal: additional Gen-2 artwork, including Hisuian Arcanine. See POLISHED-CREDITS.md and POLISHED-README.md for contributor attribution.
- PokeAPI/sprites: remaining normal/shiny fronts. Original Pokemon artwork belongs to Nintendo, Game Freak and The Pokemon Company; custom later-generation B&W-style artwork is credited to the Smogon sprite-project contributors named in POKEAPI-README.md. Individual authorship has not been guessed.
- Hisuian Arcanine overworld: PokemonWorkshop GameDataPacks, Gen 9 pack. Full generation-level artist and compilation credits and upstream terms are retained in ../community_overworld/CREDITS.md and UPSTREAM-LICENSE.md.

PROVENANCE.json records exact upstream repositories, commit IDs, file URLs and output hashes. Alternative fronts are fitted proportionally to a 56x56 frame using nearest-neighbor scaling; Gen-2 sprites retain their source dimensions. Shiny palettes from the Gen-2 repositories are applied to their two authored shades.

Hisuian Arcanine's missing record uses PokeAPI's documented form data and Scarlet/Violet learnset. It is an evolution target; no new biome spawn entry was added. Espurr resolves to the existing male/female Meowstic records using its gender. These two source gaps were filled with the user's explicit approval.
