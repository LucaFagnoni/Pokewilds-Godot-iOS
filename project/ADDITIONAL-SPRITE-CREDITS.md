## Online trainer front portraits

- Existing Gym Leader and Elite Four portraits retain their original source-pack credits.
- Red, Leaf, Ethan/Gold, Kris, Brendan, May, Hilbert, Hilda, Rosa and Rocket grunt front sprites: original Pokémon game artwork, distributed by the Pokémon Showdown trainer-sprite directory: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/
- Calem, Chase, Elaine and Gloria: **Brumirage**, as credited in that directory. Serena: **kyledove**. Ash is supplied by the same directory, which does not list a separate artist for that entry.
- Wes: **Solo993**, based on **tebited15**; original artist permits use with both credits: https://www.deviantart.com/solo993/art/Pokemon-Colosseum-Wes-trainer-sprite-467416301
- Mark and Mint: original Pokémon Trading Card Game 2 opponent portraits. Lunick, Kellyn and Summer: original Pokémon Ranger front sprites. Source archive: https://archives.bulbagarden.net/ (TCG2 Mark opponent, TCG2 Mint opponent, Ra Lunick f, Soa Kellyn ra f, Gs Summer f).
- Sapphire: **ThanatosZero**, from the Gen 3 Overhaul pack. Gardenia: front artwork from that same pack; original pack credits retained. https://github.com/BansheeStudio/PokemonWildsGen3Overhaul
- Plushbanshee and Starbutterfarts: new generated front portraits made with the project owner's authorization, referenced to the existing character sheets. Original pack credits: **BanBan OwO**; Plushbanshee also credits **SaintBanBan (WIP)**.
- Front portraits are sized and recolored at runtime for the selected player color. Original source images are retained.
