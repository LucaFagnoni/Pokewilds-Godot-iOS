Rare legendary encounter artwork

Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Raikou, Entei, Suicune and Celebi use the normal and shiny walking sheets, battle animation sheets, back sprites, palettes and cries already supplied in this project's source assets.
Arceus likewise uses its existing battle animation sheet, back sprite, shiny battle palette and cry.

Arceus walking artwork and normal/shiny palettes:
rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion
https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/tree/master/graphics/pokemon/arceus
Source files: overworld.png, overworld_normal.pal, overworld_shiny.pal.
Source animation layout: src/data/object_events/object_event_anims.h (two-frame south, north and west; east mirrors west).
Converted to the game's west/north/south six-frame layout, with palette background transparency and nearest-neighbor reduction to 32-pixel cells. No new poses were invented.
